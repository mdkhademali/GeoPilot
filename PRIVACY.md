# Privacy
- Notes, bookmarks, selections, tags, collections and settings are stored in `chrome.storage.local` on your device only.
- Coordinates, spectral values and GeoJSON are processed locally.
- No telemetry, analytics, accounts or API keys.
- The only external request: when you press **Look up** in DOI lookup, the DOI is sent to `https://api.crossref.org`. Crossref's own privacy terms apply.
- Page capture reads the title, URL, selected text and standard meta tags of the active tab only when you click a save action.
- Links you open (resources, DOIs) are ordinary browser navigations to third-party sites.
- Export/backup files are created locally; you control where they go. "Clear all data" erases everything stored by GeoPilot.
